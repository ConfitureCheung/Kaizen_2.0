Canvas (drag boxes here, then connect them)

Drag a column or an aggregate chip in here to create a box.
Then drag from a box's right-hand dot to another box's left-hand dot to connect them.
Drag the ( ) chip in here to create a bracket group.

Each connecting line carries its own calculation tool right in the middle — click the green circle on a line to cycle through + − × ÷ ^ %, and click the small × beside it to remove that connection. Click a box's × to delete it and its connections. Drop ABS/ROUND/SQRT directly onto a box to wrap it; drop a column onto an aggregate box to fill it in. Each box can have at most one line in and one line out, so the whole graph reads as one ordered chain (evaluated left-to-right with no operator precedence).

Brackets: drag the ( ) chip onto the canvas to create a dashed group, then drag column/aggregate chips into it (or drag an existing box in or out to move it between the group and the main canvas — this clears that box's connections). Connect boxes inside the group to form its own inner chain; the group's own left/right dots then let you wire the whole bracketed result into the outer chain, just like real parentheses. Drop a function chip on the group's dashed border to wrap the whole group in ABS/ROUND/SQRT. Groups can't be nested inside another group.
